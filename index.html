Who We Are

A Rising Force for Women Veterans

Up To Here, Inc. is a dedicated nonprofit empowering women veterans who have served our nation with honor and courage. We stand alongside these remarkable individuals as they transition back to civilian life.

"Our mission is simple yet profound — to create a transformative journey through holistic wellness and peer-to-peer support."

As a new startup with a big dream, we are building a strong, supportive community where women veterans can embrace their full potential and lead lives of purpose and fulfillment.

Why We Exist

The Pillars That Drive Us

Four core commitments guide everything we do for the women veterans we serve.

🎖️

Honoring Courage

Women veterans have displayed unmatched courage in serving our country. It is our duty to honor their sacrifices and offer unwavering support in their transition back to civilian life.

💪

Holistic Wellness

Our programs focus on the whole woman — physical, emotional, and mental well-being — nurturing overall wellness so our heroines can thrive and lead fulfilling lives.

🤝

Strength in Sisterhood

We foster a sisterhood of support, compassion, and understanding. Our peer-to-peer initiatives create a safe space where women veterans connect, heal, and grow together.

🌟

Building Resilience

With the right resources, mentorship, and encouragement, women veterans can overcome any challenge and embrace their post-military journey with confidence and purpose.
