Our purpose

Service is part of your story.
Not the end of it.

Military service shapes our experiences, relationships and perspectives. Wellbeing, growth and meaningful connection matter throughout every stage of life.

Based in Houston, Texas, we welcome veterans of every gender and military branch and their families, with virtual programming available nationwide.

Our Mission

To promote the mental health and overall wellbeing of veterans and military families through education, meaningful connection, and accessible wellness experiences.

Our Vision

A community where every veteran and military family feels supported, connected, and empowered to prioritize their wellbeing.

The story behind our mission

Rooted in service.
Committed to wellbeing.

After more than three decades of military service, I understand that the experiences that shape us extend far beyond our time in uniform.

I founded Up To Here, Inc. to create opportunities for veterans and military families to strengthen their wellbeing, build meaningful connections and continue growing through every stage of life.

As a retired Army Reserve Master Sergeant and Mental Health First Aid Instructor, I believe education, community and connection can make a meaningful difference in how we support ourselves and one another.

Up To Here is an invitation to continue the journey — together.

Kisha White
Founder · Retired Army Reserve Master Sergeant · MHFA Instructor

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a mental health background?

No. The course is designed to teach practical first aid skills to adults.

What will I learn?

Recognizing common signs and symptoms, responding to crises, connecting people with appropriate help, and self-care.

How do I prepare for the virtual class?

Complete the assigned self-paced learning before class and arrange a reliable internet connection. Contact us for accessibility or technology questions.

Is this therapy or crisis counseling?

No. MHFA is an educational program, not clinical treatment or emergency support.
